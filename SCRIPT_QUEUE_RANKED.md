## Scoring Methodology

Each script scored on 5 factors derived from what's actually performing on @sirakinb's TikTok:

| Factor | Weight | What It Measures |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **Hook Strength** | 25% | Does it match proven hook patterns? (opinion question, hard number, breaking news, "stop doing this") |
| **Format Match** | 25% | Is it in the 45-75s sweet spot? Does it follow opinion+tutorial hybrid format? |
| **Comment Potential** | 20% | Will people argue/share their answer? (top driver of algorithmic reach) |
| **Builder Identity Fit** | 15% | Does it reinforce "builder who shares what he knows" vs. generic tech creator? |
| **Shareability** | 15% | Would someone send this to a friend or save it? |

**Score Range:** 1-10 per factor, weighted total out of 10.

## Brand Alignment Notes

Every script should pass this filter before filming:

**Does this sound like a builder sharing what he knows, or a coach selling a course?**

- Builder: "Here's what happened when I built X" / "I use Claude for building, ChatGPT for thinking"
- Coach: "Here's what you SHOULD do" / "Follow these 5 steps to success"

Your top-performing videos are the ones where you sound like a peer sharing real experience, not an authority dispensing advice. Keep that energy.
