**Key stat:** 50% retention in first 6 seconds = viral (1M+). 35M views in a few months.

## Top Hook Patterns Most Relevant to @sirakinb Content

### PATTERN 1: Hard Number + Outcome
- "[number] things about [niche] i wish i knew earlier"
- "[number] steps to achieve [outcome] that brings [benefit]"
- "[number] hacks to supercharge your [specific area] game!"
- "use these [number] simple steps to 10x your [skill] without [common barrier]"
- "here's a [number] step formula to [achieve result] faster"

### PATTERN 2: Myth Killer / Contrarian
- "[common practice] is dead. stop focusing on it for [desired outcome]."
- "[niche topic] is bullshit."
- "everything you knew about [subject] is wrong!"
- "turns out everything we knew about [topic] was wrong."
- "here's why [common advice] is completely wrong."
- "you're doing [something] wrong—here's how to do it right"
- "you've been lied to about [topic]!"

### PATTERN 3: Urgency / Warning
- "[target audience] is screwed."
- "we're screwed."
- "this [pain point] could ruin [desired result] instantly."
- "this mistake is going to cost you millions..."
- "if you're not [desired result], you're falling behind"
- "stop doing [specific thing]."
- "you need to stop doing this immediately."

### PATTERN 4: Story / Experience
- "i set up [action] before bed and woke up to [result]"
- "i tried [method] for [time frame], and here's what happened"
- "i was working at [job] with only $[amount] in my bank account. here's how i turned it around."
- "how i went from [past situation] to [desired result] in just [time] years"
- "i went from 0 to [achievement] in [time frame]"

### PATTERN 5: Secret / Insider Knowledge
- "nobody's talking about this, but..."
- "[industry] will hate me for exposing this."
- "the dark secret behind [industry practice]."
- "exposing my secret to [desired result]"
- "i'll tell you a secret no one has ever told you about [insider knowledge]."
- "my clients pay $xxx to learn this (today it's free)"

### PATTERN 6: "Stop Doing This"
- "stop doing [common mistake] now! instead, [alternative solution]."
- "as a [niche], please stop making this mistake."
- "3 things you need to stop doing right now"
- "don't copy and paste from chatgpt. you need to humanize the writing first."
- "if you're still [doing this], i'm begging you to listen to me"

### PATTERN 7: Question / Challenge
- "98% of [niche] gets this wrong (maybe you do?)"
- "99% of [target audience] do not understand this!"
- "are you still [common action]?"
- "why are you still [doing this] in [year]?"
- "what if i told you there's a simple way to [desired result]"

### PATTERN 8: This Changed Everything
- "this changed everything for me..."
- "this hack will save you hours if you [related to niche]."
- "this is the only thing you need to know about [topic]"
- "this free tool is a game-changer!"
- "[desired outcome] is not hard anymore."

### PATTERN 9: Famous Person / Authority
- "[famous person] makes millions of dollars a month, and we're about to steal it, together, with ai."
- "this guy makes $x million a year by [specific method] and we're gonna expose his entire [strategy]."
- "[famous person] shut down their [business] to go all in on [new venture]."

### PATTERN 10: Cost / Money
- "here's a side hustle for the broke teenagers."
- "your $[amount]/month [subscription] isn't an expense — it's [reframe]"
- "i lost [$x,xxx] doing [niche topic]."
- "you're losing $xxx+ every month. but there is a solution:"
- "how to [achieve goal] for free"

## Visual Hooks Most Relevant to Talking Head

1. Lean towards the camera
2. Hold microphone in your hand
3. Walking while talking
4. Tap the camera
5. Zoom into your face in editing
6. Show something unusual
7. Have something unusual in the background
8. Write something down
9. Act like you're setting up the phone
10. Jump/slide into the frame
11. Switch scenes abruptly to surprise viewers
12. Display bold text to convey key messages
13. Use backlighting to create mysterious outlines
