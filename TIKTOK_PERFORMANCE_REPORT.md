## Pattern Analysis

### WHAT WORKS (Film More of This)

**1. Opinion-Driving Questions ("What are YOU doing?")**
- Web app vs PWA vs native = **1,797 views, 40 comments**
- This is your #1 format. Asking the audience to pick a side = massive comments
- The comment prompt IS the content strategy

**2. Breaking News + Hot Takes (With Stakes)**
- Pentagon/Anthropic = **1,566 views, 15 shares**
- Jack Dorsey layoffs = **930 views, 6 shares**
- RentAHuman = **1,105 views, 7 shares**
- Key: it's not just news — it's news with a **clear opinion** and **stakes** people care about

**3. "Demystifier" Tutorials (Save This)**
- APIs explained = **663 views, 10.3% engagement**
- Stripe integration = **638 views, 9.1% engagement**
- PRD in 20 minutes = **526 views, 11.8% engagement**
- DevOps environments = **403 views, 7.2% engagement**
- Pattern: practical, actionable, "save this" CTA, under 70 seconds

**4. Hard Number Hooks**
- "$200/month" format, "$0.13/min", "cut Cursor bill by 80%"
- Cursor bill cut = **577 views, 11 comments**
- Cost hacks consistently pull above-average engagement

**5. Builder Identity Content**
- First 10 users = **657 views**
- Mission-driven monetization = **402 views**
- Content that says "you're a builder, here's builder advice" resonates

### WHAT DOESN'T WORK (Avoid or Fix)

**1. Pure Technical Explainers Without Stakes**
- RAG 2.0 = **166 views** (no "why should I care?" hook)
- AI hallucination = **81 views** (everyone already knows this)
- Authentication = **185 views** (too tutorial, no hook)
- Fix: add stakes/consequences, not just "here's how"

**2. Long-Form Rambles (>120 seconds)**
- 176s domain expertise video = **229 views**
- 143s abundance vs purpose = **212 views**
- 153s agent era summary = **273 views**
- 215s interfaces for agents = **284 views**
- Pattern: videos over 2 minutes consistently underperform
- **Sweet spot: 40-90 seconds**

**3. No-Hashtag Posts**
- Videos without hashtags average ~270 views
- Videos with 3-5 hashtags average ~430 views
- ~60% more reach with basic hashtag hygiene

**4. Niche Service/Tool Reviews**
- GHL = **222 views** (too niche, "service business" energy)
- Insforge = **258 views** (product review without broader lesson)
- These feel like sponsored content even when they're not

**5. "Tips" Without Tension**
- Code ownership = **224 views** (no urgency in the hook)
- PRD "not fancy" = **213 views** (deflating your own hook)
- Fix: lead with the PROBLEM, not the solution

## Key Recommendations

1. **Lead with opinion-driving questions** — "What are you building?", "Which one are you on?", "Is this good or bad?" drives 2-3x the comments
2. **Keep videos 45-75 seconds** — your sweet spot for both views and engagement
3. **Always use 3-5 hashtags** — 60% more reach, no reason to skip this
4. **Post between 7-9 PM EST** — your audience is most active here
5. **Add stakes to every hook** — don't just explain, explain WHY IT MATTERS
6. **Combine formats** — opinion hook + tutorial delivery = best of both worlds
7. **Kill the "tool review" format** — unless it's a comparison or cost hack angle
8. **Double down on "builder identity" content** — your best-performing unique angle
