# Hook Mastery Guide for Coach Aki Content

## THE 3-LAYER HOOK SYSTEM (Use All Three Every Time)

Every video needs three hooks firing at once. Most people only use one -- that's why they get scrolled past.

### Layer 1: AUDIO HOOK (What you say)
Your first sentence. Lands in under 2 seconds. No warm-up, no "hey guys," no intro. Start mid-thought like you're interrupting the viewer.

### Layer 2: TEXT HOOK (What they read on screen)
Bold text on screen that reinforces or teases what you're saying. This catches people scrolling with sound off. Should be a shortened, punchier version of your audio hook.

### Layer 3: VISUAL HOOK (What they see)
Your energy, facial expression, hand gesture, or a prop. Lean in, point at camera, shake your head "no," hold up your phone showing an app. The visual pattern interrupt stops the thumb.

**Example for Script 1 "You Don't Need to Code":**
- AUDIO: "You do NOT need to learn to code to build an app in 2026."
- TEXT ON SCREEN: "you DON'T need to code" (appears word by word)
- VISUAL: Lean into camera + shake head "no" on the word "NOT"

## THE 10 HOOK FORMULAS THAT WORK IN YOUR NICHE

These are ranked by performance in the tech/AI education space in 2026. Each formula includes the pattern, why it works, and examples using your actual content topics.

---

### 1. THE MYTH KILLER
**Pattern:** "You do NOT need [thing everyone assumes] to [desirable outcome]."
**Why it works:** Contrarian statements trigger curiosity. The brain can't scroll past a challenge to its beliefs.

**Examples from your content:**
- "You do NOT need to learn to code to build an app in 2026."
- "Your app does NOT need to look pretty to make money."
- "You do NOT need a developer to launch a real product."
- "You do NOT need to be technical to automate your business."

---

### 2. THE CALLOUT
**Pattern:** "If you're [doing common thing], you're [doing it wrong / wasting time / losing money]."
**Why it works:** Self-identification. Viewer thinks "wait, I do that" and stops to find out why it's wrong.

**Examples:**
- "If your app has more than two features and you haven't launched yet, you're doing it wrong."
- "If you're burning through your Lovable credits, you're using it wrong."
- "If you're designing your app before it works, you're wasting time."
- "If you're building alone with no GitHub backup, you're one update away from losing everything."

---

### 3. THE HARD NUMBER
**Pattern:** "[Specific number/stat] + [unexpected context]."
**Why it works:** Specificity = credibility. Numbers stop the scroll because they feel concrete and provable.

**Examples:**
- "I showed a law firm how AI could answer their phones for 13 cents a minute."
- "A company just said that what used to take them 6 months now takes 1 week."
- "One bad update cost someone 10 hours of work because they didn't know about GitHub."
- "52 questions. That's how many came up across 73 real coaching calls. Here are the top 5."

---

### 4. THE "STOP DOING THIS"
**Pattern:** "Stop [common action] before [consequence]."
**Why it works:** Loss aversion. People are more motivated to avoid pain than gain pleasure. Negative hooks outperform positive ones on TikTok.

**Examples:**
- "Stop adding features to your app. Seriously."
- "Stop using Lovable credits to think. That's what ChatGPT is for."
- "Stop trying to automate a process you haven't written down yet."
- "Stop waiting for your app to be perfect. It never will be."

---

### 5. THE DEMYSTIFIER
**Pattern:** "Let me explain [scary/confusing thing] in a way that actually makes sense."
**Why it works:** Relief. The viewer has been avoiding this topic because it feels overwhelming. You're offering clarity in seconds.

**Examples:**
- "Let me explain GitHub in a way that actually makes sense."
- "Let me explain what a database actually does in 15 seconds."
- "Let me explain the difference between Lovable, Replit, and Cursor."
- "Let me explain what deploying an app actually means."

---

### 6. THE "NOBODY TALKS ABOUT THIS"
**Pattern:** "Nobody's talking about [insider knowledge / hidden problem]."
**Why it works:** Exclusivity. Viewer feels like they're about to get insider information that gives them an edge.

**Examples:**
- "Nobody's talking about why apps built with AI tools keep breaking."
- "Nobody tells you this about launching an app: building it is only half the job."
- "Nobody warns you about the cost of running an app after you build it."
- "Nobody talks about the real reason your app has zero downloads."

---

### 7. THE QUESTION FLIP
**Pattern:** "[Common question people ask] -- here's the real answer."
**Why it works:** Directly addresses a question the viewer has already been thinking about. Instant relevance.

**Examples:**
- "Lovable, Replit, or Cursor -- which one should you actually use?"
- "'When is my app ready to launch?' Wrong question. Here's the right one."
- "'Should I build a native app or a PWA?' Let me save you months."
- "'How do I get my first users?' Here's what nobody tells you."

---

### 8. THE STORY OPEN
**Pattern:** "I was [on a call / working with someone / in a meeting] and [unexpected moment]."
**Why it works:** Narrative tension. The brain is wired to follow stories to their conclusion.

**Examples:**
- "I was on a coaching call and someone told me they'd been building their app for 5 months. It had 10 features. And zero users."
- "A 76-year-old woman showed up to my program and said 'I want to build an app.' Here's what happened."
- "I got on a call with a law firm that was spending thousands on a call center. I showed them something that cost 13 cents a minute."
- "Someone came to me with two app ideas and couldn't pick one. I asked them one question that settled it immediately."

---

### 9. THE "HERE'S WHAT I TELL EVERYONE"
**Pattern:** "Every [person I work with / client / student] asks me [question]. Here's what I always say."
**Why it works:** Authority + universality. If EVERYONE asks it, the viewer feels validated. Your answer positions you as the expert.

**Examples:**
- "Every person I work with asks me 'which platform should I build on?' Here's what I tell them."
- "Every new app builder makes the same mistake. They try to build 10 features before launching one."
- "Every service business owner asks me 'can AI really handle my phone calls?' Let me show you."
- "Everybody I coach asks me 'do I need to learn to code?' The answer might surprise you."

---

### 10. THE URGENCY FRAME
**Pattern:** "[Thing] is changing [faster than you think / right now] and [consequence of not acting]."
**Why it works:** FOMO. The window is closing. Creates immediate emotional tension.

**Examples:**
- "AI is moving so fast that what took 6 months last year now takes a week."
- "Right now, you can build an app without code. This window won't be open forever."
- "The people learning AI tools today will have a massive advantage over everyone else in 12 months."
- "Every week a new AI model drops and makes the last one look slow. Are you keeping up?"

## HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

### For Talking Head Videos (Primary Format):
1. Pick a topic from the 52 questions
2. Choose 1-2 hook formulas from above that fit the topic
3. Write your opening sentence using the formula
4. Add your text-on-screen version (shorter, punchier)
5. Plan your visual hook (lean in, point, hold up phone, etc.)

### For Screen Share Videos (When Inspired):
- Use hook formulas 3, 5, or 7 (numbers, demystify, question flip)
- Say the hook to camera FIRST, then cut to screen share
- Text on screen: "watch this" or "let me show you"

### For Impromptu / Raw Content:
- Use hook formulas 4, 6, or 8 (stop doing this, nobody talks about, story open)
- Raw energy sells. Don't overthink it.
- Film in the moment when you're fired up about something

### For Carousel / Text Posts (Phase 2):
- Hook formulas 2, 3, 7, and 9 translate best to text
- The hook becomes your first slide / first line
- Each slide = one key point from the body of the script
- Last slide = CTA

## HOOK PERFORMANCE RULES FOR 2026

1. **1.5-second rule**: Your hook must land in under 1.5 seconds. The old 3-second window has tightened.
2. **No intros**: Never start with "Hey guys" or "So today I wanted to talk about." Start IN the content.
3. **Energy at 120%**: Deliver the hook at 20% higher energy than your normal talking voice. You can settle into your natural tone after the hook.
4. **Sound-off test**: If someone watches your video on mute, does the text on screen make them turn the sound on? If not, your text hook is weak.
5. **Authenticity wins**: Raw, slightly imperfect delivery outperforms polished studio content by 60% on TikTok in 2026. Your teleprompter + own spin approach is the right call.
6. **A/B test hooks**: Film the same script with two different hooks. Post both at different times. See which one holds viewers longer. The algorithm will tell you which works.
