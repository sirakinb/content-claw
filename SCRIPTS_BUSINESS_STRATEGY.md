# Business & Strategy — Scripts
**Source:** Coaching calls, community calls, recurring questions
**Style:** Builder sharing what he knows | **Length:** 30-90 sec | **Format:** Talking head (teleprompter)
**Primary Platform:** TikTok (cross-post to IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn)

**Every script includes the 3-Layer Hook System:**
- **AUDIO HOOK** = What you say (first sentence, under 1.5 seconds)
- **TEXT ON SCREEN** = What they read (shorter/punchier, catches sound-off scrollers)
- **VISUAL HOOK** = What they see (your energy, gesture, expression)

**Teleprompter formatting:** Short sentences. **Bold** = words to punch with energy. Read naturally, not robotically.

**Quick Reference:**
- Bold words = punch with energy (louder or pause before)
- Hook gets 120% energy, then settle into natural tone
- Each line break = natural breath point
- Don't be perfect. Raw > polished.

## SCRIPT 9: "Balancing Building With a Full-Time Job"
**Category:** Mindset | **Hook:** Here's What I Tell Everyone | **Length:** 45-60 sec
**Status:** NOT YET FILMED

**HOOK:**
- AUDIO: "You have a job, a life, and an app idea. Here's how to balance all **three**."
- TEXT ON SCREEN: "building an app with a 9-5? here's how"
- VISUAL: Lean back slightly, then come in close. Relatable, empathetic energy -- you get it.

**CAPTION:**
You don't need 4 hours a night. You need 30 focused minutes. The people who ship apps aren't finding more time -- they're using small pockets more intentionally.

#sideproject #buildanapp #vibecoding #appdevelopment #9to5 #mindset

**SCRIPT:**

You have a job. You have a life.
You have an app idea.
How do you balance all **three**?

**First.** Stop trying to find big blocks of time.
They don't **exist**.
Use thirty-minute **windows**.
Before work. Lunch break.
After the kids are **asleep**.

**Second.** Batch your work.
Monday is **planning**.
Tuesday is **building**.
Wednesday is **testing**.
Don't try to do everything in one **session**.

**Third.** Set a **weekly** goal. Not a daily one.
Some days you'll get an **hour**.
Some days you'll get ten **minutes**.
That's fine.

**Progress** is progress.

The people who ship apps with full-time jobs
aren't finding **more** time.
They're using small pockets of time
more **intentionally**.
