## RECURRING QUESTIONS (Content Gold)

These are questions that came up across MULTIPLE meetings or represent themes so consistent they're clearly on people's minds. These are organized by topic and ranked by how frequently they appeared.

### GETTING STARTED WITH VIBE CODING

1. **Which platform should I build on -- Lovable, Replit, or Cursor?**
   - Came up in 15+ meetings. Everyone asks this. The answer evolves based on skill level.

2. **How do I get started building an app if I have zero coding experience?**
   - Nearly every onboarding call starts here. People are intimidated but excited.

3. **What is vibe coding, and how is it different from traditional coding?**
   - Implied in almost every conversation. People are still wrapping their heads around this.

4. **Do I need to learn to code to build an app in 2026?**
   - The answer is no, and people are shocked by what's possible.

5. **What is a PRD and why do I need one before I start building?**
   - Comes up in framework discussions and onboarding calls.

### THE MVP TRAP

6. **What features should be in my MVP vs. what should wait?**
   - Came up in 20+ meetings. This is the #1 mistake people make -- trying to build everything at once.

7. **How do I stop adding features and actually launch?**
   - Perfectionism is killing people's progress. Multiple clients stuck in build mode.

8. **When is my app "ready" to launch?**
   - Tyneshia, Alex Harris, Vicki, Joyce, and many others asked this directly or implied it.

9. **Should I focus on design or functionality first?**
   - Recurring tension. Aki consistently says functionality first, design second.

10. **I have two (or more) app ideas -- how do I choose which one to build?**
    - Kareemah, Shane, Laquita, M Lynn, Edith, and others all struggled with this.

### GITHUB & VERSION CONTROL

11. **What is GitHub and why do I need it?**
    - Came up in virtually every technical session. Aki describes it as "Google Drive for your projects."

12. **How do I save/backup my work from Lovable or Replit to GitHub?**
    - One of the most asked technical questions across all meetings.

13. **I lost hours of work because I didn't save properly -- how do I prevent that?**
    - Alex Harris lost 10+ hours. Multiple others had similar experiences.

14. **How do I revert to a previous version of my app when something breaks?**
    - Common fear: making a change that breaks everything with no way back.

### PLATFORM MIGRATION & TOOLS

15. **How do I move my app from Lovable to Cursor (or Replit to Cursor)?**
    - The natural progression question as apps outgrow beginner platforms.

16. **How do I connect different platforms together through GitHub?**
    - Carmella, multiple tech calls. GitHub as the intermediary is a key concept.

17. **I'm burning through my Lovable credits -- how do I build without running out?**
    - Extremely common. Aki recommends using ChatGPT/Google AI Studio for thinking, Lovable for building.

18. **What's the difference between Lovable, Replit, Cursor, and Bolt?**
    - Asked by customers, relayed through Shallon, and discussed in multiple group calls.

### DEPLOYMENT & GOING LIVE

19. **How do I deploy my app so people can actually use it?**
    - Confusion about Vercel, Replit hosting, Lovable hosting, and custom domains.

20. **How do I connect a custom domain to my app?**
    - Tech call question. People don't understand DNS/hosting.

21. **How do I get my app on the Apple App Store or Google Play?**
    - Multiple people asked. Apple rejected Emmanuel's app multiple times.

22. **What is a PWA and should I build one instead of a native app?**
    - Dawn Green, Shane Turner, Tyneshia, and others. PWA explained repeatedly.

### PAYMENT & MONETIZATION

23. **How do I connect Stripe to my app for payments?**
    - Alex Harris, Vicki, and multiple tech call participants asked this.

24. **How should I price my app -- subscription, freemium, or one-time purchase?**
    - Dawn Green, Crystle, Joyce, and others struggled with pricing strategy.

25. **How do I monetize my app without pricing out the people I'm trying to help?**
    - Dawn Green (neurodivergent families), Emma (bullied students) -- mission-driven founders face this tension.

26. **What are the ongoing costs of running an app (hosting, API, database)?**
    - Partner Plus call, tech calls. People don't understand the cost of scale.

### DATABASE & DATA ISSUES

27. **Why isn't my data saving properly (local storage vs. database)?**
    - Alex Harris's biggest issue. Common with Replit apps.

28. **How do I set up a database for my app?**
    - Supabase, Lovable cloud backend, and Replit databases all discussed.

29. **How do I fix sign-up/authentication issues?**
    - Jean P Alce Saint (broken web sign-up), Emma (missing buttons), Joyce (CORS errors).

### TESTING & QUALITY

30. **How do I test my app to make sure it actually works?**
    - Gwendolyn is literally building an app to solve this problem. Asson brings QA expertise. Major gap.

31. **My app works on my computer but not on other browsers/devices -- what do I do?**
    - Cross-browser compatibility came up in tech calls.

32. **How do I handle bugs that keep coming back after I fix them?**
    - Emma Blackshire: features reappearing after removal. A common frustration.

### SECURITY & SCALING

33. **Is my vibe-coded app secure enough for real users?**
    - Asson raised KYC/data privacy concerns. Aki discussed security audit companies (ResLiant, SNC).

34. **Can a vibe-coded app actually scale to thousands of users?**
    - The "0 to 1 is easy, 1 to 10 is hard" theme from the framework discussions.

35. **How do I set up proper development, staging, and production environments?**
    - Emmanuel crashed production by running tests in a live environment. A cautionary tale.

### BUSINESS STRATEGY

36. **Should I build from passion or from market demand?**
    - Justin Burns hammers this: build for a market, not just your passion.

37. **How do I validate my app idea before spending months building it?**
    - Customer discovery, talking to potential users, testing assumptions.

38. **How do I do an elevator pitch for my app?**
    - Accountability calls. People can't articulate what their app does in 2 minutes.

39. **How do I get my first users/customers?**
    - The bootcamp Day 3 focused entirely on this. 99% of people build and nobody buys.

40. **How do I market my app if I'm not a marketer?**
    - Distribution, storytelling, collecting testimonials, pre-selling.

### AUTOMATION & WORKFLOWS

41. **How do I automate my business processes with AI?**
    - Heaven Solutions (tax), Tyrone (daily reports), Jackson (property management).

42. **How do I map out my existing workflow before automating it?**
    - You can't automate what you haven't defined. Came up with Tyrone and Heaven.

43. **What tools should I use for automation (Zapier, Make.com, n8n)?**
    - Platform comparison for automation tools, similar to vibe coding platform comparison.

44. **Can AI handle phone calls and intake for my business?**
    - White Law AI voice/SMS intake demos. 13-14 cents/minute vs. call center costs.

### AI & EMERGING TECH

45. **How do I use AI without it hallucinating or giving wrong information?**
    - BronxTechUp interview. AI hallucination with math/calculations.

46. **How fast is AI actually changing, and how do I keep up?**
    - "6 months of work now done in 1 week." New models every few weeks.

47. **What's the difference between ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini?**
    - Aki shifting from ChatGPT to Claude. Multiple discussions about which AI to use.

48. **Can AI agents actually replace employees or parts of my workflow?**
    - AI social media agents, AI phone agents, AI intake assistants.

### MINDSET & ACCOUNTABILITY

49. **How do I stay focused when I have shiny object syndrome?**
    - M Lynn literally wrote a book about this. Multiple participants struggle with focus.

50. **How do I balance building an app with my full-time job and life?**
    - Laquita (night-shift caregiver + realtor), Edith (solo operator), Dana (family tragedy).

51. **How do I push past the fear of launching something imperfect?**
    - Perfectionism is the silent killer. Multiple people stuck because it's "not ready."

52. **I'm building alone and it's overwhelming -- how do I get support?**
    - Community, accountability partners, and peer support are consistently valued.

## CONTENT FORMAT SUGGESTIONS FOR SHORT-FORM

- "You don't need to code to build an app in 2026"
- "Stop using Lovable credits to think -- use ChatGPT instead"
- "GitHub explained in 15 seconds"

**Story-Based Content (30-60 seconds):**
- Client transformation stories (with permission): Alex the veteran, Dawn building for neurodivergent families, Emma fighting school bullying
- "A 76-year-old retired woman is building an app. What's your excuse?"
- "This nurse automated her paperwork nightmare with AI"

**Educational Series:**
- "Vibe Coding 101" series
- "Platform Showdown" (Lovable vs Replit vs Cursor)
- "From Idea to MVP" step-by-step
- "Automation in 60 Seconds" series

**Myth Busters:**
- "You need a developer to build an app" -- FALSE
- "AI-built apps can't scale" -- addressing the concern
- "You need to know which features to build first" -- start with the user journey
- "Building from passion is enough" -- you need market demand too
