# Business Strategy & Monetization — Scripts
**Source:** Recurring questions from 73 coaching calls + 9 community sessions
**Style:** Builder sharing what he knows | **Length:** 30-90 sec | **Format:** Talking head (teleprompter)
**Primary Platform:** TikTok (cross-post to IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn)

**Every script includes the 3-Layer Hook System:**
- **AUDIO HOOK** = What you say (first sentence, under 1.5 seconds)
- **TEXT ON SCREEN** = What they read (shorter/punchier, catches sound-off scrollers)
- **VISUAL HOOK** = What they see (your energy, gesture, expression)

**Teleprompter formatting:** Short sentences. **Bold** = words to punch with energy. Read naturally, not robotically.

**Quick Reference:**
- Bold words = punch with energy (louder or pause before)
- Hook gets 120% energy, then settle into natural tone
- Each line break = natural breath point
- Don't be perfect. Raw > polished.

## SCRIPT 1: "How to Monetize a Mission-Driven App Without Pricing People Out"
**Category:** Business Strategy | **Hook:** Question Flip | **Length:** 45-60 sec
**Status:** NOT YET FILMED

**HOOK:**
- AUDIO: "How do you make **money** from an app that's supposed to **help** people?"
- TEXT ON SCREEN: "mission-driven app? here's how you monetize it"
- VISUAL: Lean in with open hands. Genuine expression -- this is a real tension you care about.

**CAPTION:**
You don't have to choose between impact and income. You design a model that does both. Who's building something that matters?

#missiondriven #appmonetization #vibecoding #buildanapp #socialimpact #startup

**SCRIPT:**

How do you make **money** from an app that's supposed to **help** people?

This is the tension every mission-driven founder faces.

You're building for underserved communities.
Neurodivergent families. Bullied students. People who **need** the tool
but can't always **pay** for it.

Here's the answer. **Tiered pricing.**

Free tier for the people who need it **most**.
Paid tier for organizations, schools, and businesses that **can** afford it.

Or a freemium model --
the core value is **free**. Premium features are paid.

Or find sponsors and grants that **subsidize** access.

Stop thinking you have to choose between **impact** and **income**.
Design a model that serves **both**.

The people who need it most get it free.
The people and organizations who can pay -- **do**.

**CTA:** "Tag someone building something that matters. Let's help them monetize it right."

- AUDIO: "Here's the three-tier pricing model every vibe coder should know."
- TEXT ON SCREEN: "how to price your vibe coding services"
- VISUAL: Hold up three fingers. Confident, teaching energy. Slight nod like "pay attention."

**CAPTION:**
Three tiers. Three income streams. Start with Tier 3 and work your way up. Which tier are you at?

#vibecoding #pricing #freelancing #buildanapp #saas #businessstrategy

**SCRIPT:**

Here's the three-tier pricing model every vibe coder should know.

**Tier one.** SaaS product.
You build it once. Charge a monthly subscription.
Users self-serve. This **scales** --
but it takes time to build and market.

**Tier two.** Done-with-you.
You clone your repo, customize it for the client,
teach them how to use it.
Charge **one to five thousand** dollars.

**Tier three.** Done-for-you.
You build the **whole** thing for the client. Custom project.
Charge **five to twenty-five thousand** dollars or more.

Now here's the move most people get wrong.

They try to build a SaaS from **day one** with no income.

Start with **tier three** -- done-for-you client work.
Generate **revenue**.
Then use that money to fund your **own** SaaS product.

Client work is the **bridge**.
Not the destination.

**CTA:** "Which tier are you at right now? Drop it in the comments."

That's not your race.

**Consistency** beats intensity.

Thirty minutes a day for six months
will get you **further** than a weekend binge
followed by three weeks of **nothing**.

**Hosting.** Vercel, Netlify --
free tier covers small apps, but it scales with traffic.

**Database.** Supabase free tier is generous,
but heavy usage costs **money**.

OpenAI, Anthropic, whatever --
that's a **charge**.

**SSL** certificate. Usually free through hosting.
**Auth** provider costs at scale.

The total can be **zero** dollars a month for a small app
or **five hundred plus** a month for a scaled one.

Here's the rule.
Know your costs **before** you set your pricing.

Otherwise you'll be making sales
and **losing** money at the same time.

Use client work to **fund** your own product development.

Then you take that knowledge
and build a product that serves that **same** market.

Client work is the **bridge**.
Not the destination.
