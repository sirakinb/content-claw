# Content Rules for @sirakinb Scripts

These are hard rules for writing TikTok scripts, captions, hooks, and cover titles in this repo. Follow them every time without being reminded.

## Banned phrases (never use, any variation)
- "Here's why this actually matters"
- "Here's the part that matters"
- Any close variant ("here's what actually matters," "this is the part that matters," "the number that matters most," etc.)

## Banned punctuation
- No em dashes (—) and no double-hyphen dashes (--) anywhere in scripts, captions, hooks, or cover titles. Use periods, commas, or a line break instead.

## Formatting
- Teleprompter format: **bold** = punch words, line breaks = breath points.
- 60-75 sec sweet spot, longer only for genuinely dense topics.
- Every script gets: AUDIO hook, TEXT ON SCREEN, COVER TITLE, VISUAL, CAPTION, SCRIPT, CTA.
- Audio hook, on-screen text, and cover title must each be a distinct angle. Never copies of each other.
- Caption must have its own standalone hook. Never restate the script or the hook.
- No preamble segues ("before I start," "I want to mention"). Credibility lines tie directly to the hook as proof ("I know, because...").

## Accuracy
- Verify facts before writing them into a script, especially dollar figures, rankings, benchmarks, dates, and sequence of events. Hedge anything unconfirmed as "reportedly," or cut it.
- If a claim the user gives can't be verified, say so before writing, and build the script around what is verified.
- Company-reported numbers (revenue, usage, benchmarks) get labeled as the company's own claims.
- Financial content (stocks, IPOs, trading) never predicts price or says whether to buy; add a short "not financial advice" line.
- When the user supplies reference material (e.g. another creator's video) for context only, don't cite or name that person.
